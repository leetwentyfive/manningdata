Predicting Major League Baseball Outcomes Based on Which Team Scores First

Overview

How much does scoring first actually influence the outcome of a Major League Baseball game? This project analyzes more than 65,000 historical MLB games to evaluate how the timing and margin of initial leads affect win probability.

Research Questions

  • Does scoring first in a Major League Baseball game increase a team's chances of winning?
  • Can game outcomes be predicted based on the inning and size of the first lead?

Key Findings

  • The team that scores first wins approximately two-thirds of MLB games.
  • Early scoring provides a measurable advantage, but win probability depends heavily on inning and run differential.
  • Machine learning predictions were cautious in the early innings but increasingly reflected historical expectations as games progressed.
